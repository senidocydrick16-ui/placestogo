Bantayan Island

Why You Should Visit

Bantayan Island is a stunning tropical paradise located at the northernmost tip of Cebu. It is famous for its crystal-clear waters, powdery white sand beaches, serene atmosphere, and fresh seafood. It is an ideal destination for beachgoers looking to escape the crowded tourist spots.
