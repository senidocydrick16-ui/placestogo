Bantayan Island, Cebu

Why You Should Visit

Bantayan Island is a tropical paradise located at the northernmost tip of Cebu. It is famous for its powder-white sand beaches, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and relaxed island vibe. It is an ideal destination for beach lovers, cliff jumpers, and travelers looking for a peaceful escape.
