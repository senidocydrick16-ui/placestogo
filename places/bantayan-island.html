Bantayan Island, Cebu

Why You Should Visit

Bantayan Island is a tranquil tropical paradise located at the northernmost tip of Cebu. Unlike other bustling tourist destinations, Bantayan maintains a laid-back, serene atmosphere perfect for relaxation. It is world-renowned for its powdery white sand beaches, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and stunning sunsets. Beyond its coastline, visitors can explore cliff-diving spots, historic churches, and vibrant local night markets offering fresh seafood.
