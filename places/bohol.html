Bohol Province

Why You Should Visit

Bohol is a top eco-tourism destination in the Visayas, famous for the iconic Chocolate Hills, a geological formation of over 1,000 cone-shaped hills. It is also home to the Philippine Tarsier, one of the world's smallest primates, and offers scenic river cruises along the tranquil Loboc River.
