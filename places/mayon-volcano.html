Mayon Volcano, Albay

Why You Should Visit

Mayon Volcano, located in Albay, Bicol, is world-renowned for its majestic and almost perfect symmetrical cone shape. It is one of the Philippines' most active volcanoes and serves as a backdrop to rich cultural heritage, exciting ATV adventures, and famous spicy local delicacies like Bicol Express and Chili Ice Cream.
