My Top Picks

Bantayan Island Cebu

Beaches, Cebu

Bantayan Island is known for its rich culture and extraordinary natural beauty. At its northern tip lies Bantayan Island—a perfect destination for people who love crystal-clear waters and fine white sand beaches.

Mayon Volcano (Bicol)

Destination, Albay, Bicol

province of Albay in the Bicol region is famous for its rich history and delicious, spicy cuisine. It is also home to the world-renowned Mayon Volcano, celebrated for its majestic, near-perfect cone shape.

Bohol

Eco-Tourism Destination, Bohol

The island province of Bohol takes pride in its breathtaking natural wonders and serene charm. It is home to the iconic Chocolate Hills, the adorable Philippine Tarsier, and the peaceful Loboc River.
